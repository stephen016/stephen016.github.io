## About me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en/) and [CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security](https://cispa.de/), supervised by [Prof. Michael Backes](https://michaelbackes.eu/group.html). I work daily with [Franziska Boenisch](https://franziska-boenisch.de/) and [Adam Dziedzic](https://adam-dziedzic.com/) at [SprintML Lab](https://sprintml.com/).

My research focuses on trustworthy and efficient machine learning, including privacy-preserving model adaptation and post-training, model provenance and ownership, and trustworthy world models. I study what foundation models reveal about private data and how to trace the origins of models and agents.

I received my M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from TUM in 2023, graduating with high distinction, and my B.Eng. in Vehicle Engineering from [Tongji University](https://en.tongji.edu.cn/) in 2020.

## Research interests

- **Privacy auditing and protection.** Measuring privacy leakage in foundation-model embeddings and LLM adaptations, and developing privacy-preserving prompt transfer methods.
- **Model provenance and ownership.** Auditing the identity of agentic LLM APIs, fingerprinting generative models, and protecting model intellectual property.
- **Adaptation and post-training.** Privacy-preserving and efficient adaptation of foundation models, alongside architecture-agnostic structured pruning.
- **Trustworthy world models.** Tracing whether an agent was trained in a particular world model.
