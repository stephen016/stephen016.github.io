**Xun Wang**, Adam Dziedzic, Michael Backes, Franziska Boenisch. **NeurIPS 2026**.

We study how much sensitive information can be recovered from tabular foundation-model embeddings. Our audit examines attribute recovery from embeddings alone and how additional side information changes privacy risk.
